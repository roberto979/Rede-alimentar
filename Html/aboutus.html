About Rede Alimentar

Rede Alimentar is a restaurant located in Kinaxixi, Luanda, Angola.

The restaurant offers a variety of Angolan and international dishes in a comfortable environment for individuals, families and business customers.

Rede Alimentar also provides takeaway services and catering for special events.

Our Mission

To provide customers with high-quality meals, excellent customer service, and an enjoyable dining experience while promoting fresh ingredients and traditional Angolan flavours.

Our Vision

To become one of Luanda's preferred restaurants by offering quality food, excellent service, and a modern customer experience supported by digital technology.
